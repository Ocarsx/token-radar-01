198 protocols that record no token, ranked by what their launch would be worth if their record stayed as it is. Implied value is this board's own claim, never a promise — a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry.

How the value is worked out

implied = TVL × ratio × growth × age × concentration. Ratio is a size-aware multiple fitted from the snapshot itself: the market pays fewer dollars per TVL dollar as protocols get bigger, so we fit log(mcap/TVL) against log(TVL) over the 128 token-bearing comparables and read each protocol's multiple off that line, then bend it toward its category's median multiple (only when the category has 3+ comparables, shrunk by n/(n+6)). A category with one stray comparable cannot set a price. Growth is 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −30%, +100%) / 200. Age is 0.70 + 0.15 × years listed (capped at 4 years); unlisted counts as 1.00. Concentration is 1 for the top 2 protocols of a category by TVL and 1.5/(k+1) after that — a category's launch premium concentrates in its leaders, so no single category can fill the top of the board. Nothing here names a protocol: every number comes from the snapshot's own figures.
